## [how-to-vote] How to vote | /vote
1. Create your account once at Register, using your Student ID and the voucher from the Electoral Committee, then choose a password (at least 8 characters).
2. Sign in. You go straight to your ballot.
3. Choose candidates one contest at a time. Some contests allow more than one choice, and you can abstain from any contest.
4. Review your choices, then submit. A submitted ballot cannot be changed and you cannot vote twice.
5. Keep your receipt code. It proves your ballot was recorded and never shows how you voted.
Your choices are saved in your browser tab while you vote. The ballot session lasts about 10 minutes of inactivity, and a warning appears in the last 2 minutes with a button to get more time.

## [register] Creating your account | /register
Only enrolled students on the voter roll for this election can register. You need your Student ID and the one-time voucher given by the Electoral Committee. A voucher works once. If your voucher is not accepted, check for typing mistakes, then ask the Electoral Committee for help in person. Sign-in errors are deliberately vague so that nobody can find out whether an account exists.

## [forgot-password] Forgot your password | /forgot-password
Use the Forgot password page and enter your Student ID. Follow the link you receive. If you cannot reset it, ask the Electoral Committee in person. They can issue a one-time claim code at the registration desk so you can set a new password at the Claim account page.

## [claim-account] Claim your account in person | /help/claim
If you cannot register or sign in, go to the registration desk. Staff can issue a one-time claim code. Enter it with your Student ID at the Claim account page and choose a password.

## [not-on-roll] Not on the voter roll | /election
Only currently enrolled students on the voter roll for this election can vote. If you think you should be on the roll, bring your school ID to the Electoral Committee so they can check. The assistant cannot check the roll or change it.

## [election-dates] Voting dates and status | /election
The Election page shows when voting opens and closes and whether voting is open, not yet open, paused or closed. Times are Philippine time. The home page also shows the current status.

## [candidates] Candidates | /candidates
The Candidates page shows the official profiles for the contests on your ballot after you sign in. The assistant does not recommend candidates, compare them or give opinions about them.

## [receipt] Your receipt | /receipt
After you submit, you get a receipt code. Copy, download or print it right away: it is kept only in your browser tab and is gone when you close the tab. The code does not reveal your choices. You can check that your ballot was recorded by pasting the code on the My status page. A receipt first shows as recorded and later as confirmed.

## [status] Your voting status | /status
The My status page shows whether you are eligible and whether your ballot has been recorded. It also has a box to check a receipt code. It never shows how anyone voted.

## [privacy] Ballot secrecy and privacy | /legal/privacy
Nobody can see how you voted. Draft choices stay in your browser session and are cleared when you submit, when the session expires, or when you sign out. Submitted ballots are stored encrypted and are not linked to your receipt. Your Student ID and voucher are used only to check that you are on the voter roll.

## [results] Results | /results
Results are public only after the Electoral Committee has completed certification. Until then the Results page shows only the status. Only certified results published on this site are official.

## [protest] Filing a protest | /help/protest
Use the Protest page to report a concern about the election. The Electoral Committee reviews protests within the protest period set in your organization's rules. Never include your ballot choices in a protest.

## [dispute] Someone else used my account | /help/dispute
If someone else may have registered with your Student ID, use the Account dispute page. A counted ballot cannot be identified or removed.

## [fake-site] Fake websites and messages | /help/report
Official links only come from your Electoral Committee. If you receive a suspicious website address or message about voting, report it on the Report a fake site page. Never share your password or voucher with anyone.

## [accessibility] Accessibility | /legal/accessibility
The site supports keyboard use, clear focus, high contrast, reduced motion and touch-friendly buttons. If you need help voting, contact the Electoral Committee.

## [contact] Contacting the Electoral Committee | /help/claim
For anything the assistant cannot answer, contact the Electoral Committee in person or use the help pages. The assistant is not the Electoral Committee and cannot decide or change anything.
